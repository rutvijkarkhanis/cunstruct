# IS 1200 measurement rules for building BOQs (extracted from the uploaded PDFs)

What this file is: the clauses from the 29 uploaded files that a residential or commercial building BOQ depends on. Each rule gives the clause number, the file, and the **PDF page** (the page index in the PDF, not the printed folio). Quotes are short extracts of the printed text. Numbers are copied as printed: `·` is the printed decimal point and `m²`/`m³` stand for the printed superscripts.

How the text was checked:
- Parts 2 (2025) and 11 have born-digital text, which was used directly.
- Parts 1, 3, 5, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15 and 21 are scans with OCR text. Every number quoted from them was compared with the rendered page image wherever the OCR was garbled. The Part 12 copy with no text layer was read with tesseract and checked against the other Part 12 copy.
- Part 8 is an Acrobat "Paper Capture" rendering, so no original scan image is kept. Its numbers are OCR output and some unit symbols are illegible (flagged below).

Corrections to the task's assumed part titles, confirmed from the covers:
- **Part 1 is Earthwork**, not a general-requirements part. IS 1200 has no general part. Every part repeats its own "General rules", covering clubbing, booking order, decimal measurement and rounding.
- **Part 9 is Roof Covering (including Cladding)**. Woodwork, joinery, doors and windows are in **Part 21**.
- Painting coefficients for **doors, windows, grills and gates** are in **Part 15**, Table 1. **Part 13** covers wall and ceiling surfaces: whitewash, distemper and painting of building surfaces.

Edition warnings:
- Part 2 is the 2025 Fourth Revision. Its clause 2 cites "IS 1200 (Part 5) : 2025 Formwork (fifth revision)", so the uploaded **Part 5 (1982, Third Revision) is superseded**.
- All other parts are older editions, with reaffirmations up to the dates shown in the register. The files cannot show whether BIS has revised them since.

**Deductions for plaster (3.8, PDF p. 7). Pointing (4.6, PDF pp. 9–10) uses the same wording.**
- 3.8.1(a): "No deduction shall be made for ends of joists, beams, posts, etc, and openings not exceeding 0·5 m² each and no addition shall be made for reveals, jambs, soffits, sills, etc, of these openings nor for finish to plaster around ends of joists, beams, posts, etc."
- 3.8.1(b): "Deductions for openings exceeding 0·5 m² but not exceeding 3 m² each shall be made as follows and no addition shall be made for reveals, jambs, soffits, sills, etc, of these openings:"
  - (1) "When both faces of wall are plastered with same plaster, deduction shall be made for one face only."
  - (2) Different plaster on each face, or plaster on one face and pointing on the other: deduct "on the side on which width of reveals is less … Where widths of reveals on both faces of wall are equal, deduction of 50 percent of area of opening on each face shall be made".
  - (3) One face plastered only: "full deduction shall be made from plaster if width of reveal on plastered side is less than that on unplastered side but if widths of reveal on both sides are equal or width of reveal on plastered side is more, no deduction shall be made."
  - (4) "When width of door frame is equal to thickness of wall or is projecting beyond thickness of wall, full deduction for opening shall be made from each plastered face of the wall."
- 3.8.2: "In case of openings of area above 3 m² each, deduction shall be made for opening on each face but jambs, soffits and sills shall be measured."
- NOTE: "In calculating areas of openings, the extra width of rebated reveals, if any, shall be excluded."
